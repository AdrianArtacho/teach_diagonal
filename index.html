03 / Sound

Musical notes go here on channel 1. Controller lighting uses a separate output. A browser has no guaranteed “default MIDI synth”; the built-in sound needs no extra software.

One note at a time.

Load a MIDI melody, then play the lit pad. A wrong note does not move the song forward. Repeated notes need a release and a fresh press. In “All notes” mode, collect each highlighted note in any order. Listen plays a reference; practice waits for you and does not grade rhythm.

On iPad, use touch and browser sound. For USB MIDI and Launchpad lights, use a browser with Web MIDI support, such as desktop Chrome or Edge. Files opened from your device stay in your browser; they are not uploaded.
